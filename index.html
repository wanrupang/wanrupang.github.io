I hold an MA in Chinese Culture and Language from the National University of Singapore and a BA in Chinese Language and Literature from the University of Chinese Academy of Social Sciences.

My research focuses on Chinese as a second language, with particular interests in bicultural identity integration, L2 selves, and reading and writing assessment. I combine qualitative inquiry with quantitative modelling to understand learners’ experiences and development over time.
